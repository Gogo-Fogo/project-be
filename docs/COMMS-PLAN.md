# Family comms plan

How the family reaches each other as things get worse. Use the highest layer that still works.
Save your filled-in copy as `my-comms-plan.md` (git-ignored) and print it.

## Layer 1: internet works
- Normal messaging and calls.
- **Signal** family group as a backup: end-to-end encrypted, works over any internet,
  and doesn't depend on one phone maker. Free on iPhone and Android.

## Layer 2: cell network up, data down or jammed
- **Text, don't call.** SMS often gets through when calls fail. Keep it short:
  "OK at home. Mom with me. Battery 40%."
- Pick **one out-of-area contact** (a relative in another state or country). Long-distance
  texts can work when local lines are overloaded. Everyone checks in with that person.

## Layer 3: no cell service
- **Texting by satellite:**
  - iPhone 14 or newer (iOS 18+): Messages via satellite, Emergency SOS, Find My.
  - Some recent Android phones, e.g. Pixel 9 and newer: Satellite SOS, with texting on some
    carriers.
  - All of these need a clear view of the sky. Check each phone's model and try the demo now.
- **Bluetooth mesh apps** (no internet, no account):
  - **Bitchat** (iPhone and Android) or **Briar** (Android).
  - About 30 m per phone; other phones relay. Good inside a building or block, not across town.
  - **Install them now**, while the app stores work.

## Layer 4: radios (no phones needed)
- **Walkie-talkies:**
  - FRS in the US: no license, about $30–50 a pair, roughly 0.5–2 miles in a city.
  - Other countries: PMR446 in the EU/UK, UHF CB in Australia.
  - Pick a channel now and write it below.
- *Optional:* **Meshtastic** LoRa nodes (about $25–40 each) pair with a phone app and text over
  several km with no network.

## Setup steps

**Signal** (by Signal Messenger, LLC; free)
1. Install, open, enter your number and the code it texts you. Allow notifications.
2. Create a **PIN** and keep it safe. Turn on Settings → Account → **Registration Lock**.
3. Set your profile name to the name the family knows you by.
4. Join the family group and send a test message.

**Bitchat** (free, no account; check it shows no subscription or sign-up)
1. Install, open, **allow Bluetooth** and notifications.
2. Tap the **@anon…** name at the top and type your first name.
3. Never put addresses or plans in the public chat: strangers nearby can read it. Tap a family
   member's name for a private message, and star them as a favourite.
4. Don't triple-tap the title: that erases everything (panic wipe).
5. **Test:** everyone at home turns off Wi-Fi and cellular (Bluetooth stays on) and sends one
   private message.

## Phone battery in an outage
- Low Power Mode / Battery Saver on, brightness down, camera and maps closed.
- Charge from the power station when it's in the sun.
- One phone at a time can stay on as the "listening" phone.
