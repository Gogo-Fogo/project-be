# Ready for 2 weeks

Goal: if power, water, internet or stores go down for up to 2 weeks, the household is fine at
home. The numbers are **per person**: multiply by the household size. Save your own ticked
copy as `my-ready-2-weeks.md` (git-ignored).

## 🔴 First (most important first)
- [ ] **Water: 1 gallon (4 L) per person per day × 14 days.** Refillable 5-gallon jugs or cases
      of bottled water. Fill from the tap and refresh every 6 months.
- [ ] **Medicines:** every prescription with **2+ weeks** extra. Ask the pharmacy for an early
      refill "for an emergency supply".
- [ ] **Radio:** hand-crank or battery **AM/FM** + weather band. When internet and cell service
      are down, radio is where news and official instructions come from.
      Note: a NOAA-only weather radio gives alerts, not news.
- [ ] **Light:** an LED lantern per room you'll use, a flashlight or headlamp per person, and
      spare batteries. **No candles** (fire risk).
- [ ] **Cash** in small bills. Card readers and ATMs die with the power.
- [ ] **Car:** keep the tank at least half full.
- [ ] **Power banks:** one per person, charged.

## 🍲 Food (no cooking needed)
- [ ] 2 weeks of food you can eat cold: canned beans/fish/chicken/soup, peanut butter,
      crackers, oats, nuts, dried fruit, bars, shelf-stable milk, coffee/tea, plus baby food or
      special diets.
- [ ] **Manual can opener.**
- [ ] Paper plates, cups and utensils (saves water).
- [ ] Order: fridge food first, then the freezer, then the pantry (see the power-outage card).
- [ ] Cooking without power: a camp stove **outdoors**, or briefly by an open window with a CO
      alarm on. **Never** run a charcoal grill, propane grill or generator indoors.

## 🌡️ Heat or cold
**Cold season:**
- [ ] Warm layers, hats, wool socks, sleeping bags or extra blankets.
- [ ] Live in one small room: close the other doors and cover the windows at night.
- [ ] No oven, gas stove or outdoor heater for heat (carbon monoxide).

**Hot season:**
- [ ] Shade the windows by day and ventilate at night.
- [ ] Wet cloths and spray bottles.
- [ ] Know the signs of heat illness (videos folder).

**Either season:**
- [ ] Know where the local **warming / cooling centers** are (the radio announces them).
- [ ] Battery **CO alarm**; test the smoke alarms.

## 🚽 If the water stops
- [ ] Fill the **bathtub** as soon as an outage starts, for flushing and washing.
      A tub liner (WaterBOB-style) keeps it drinkable.
- [ ] Toilet plan: heavy-duty bags + kitty litter in the bowl or a bucket.
- [ ] Wet wipes, hand sanitizer, trash bags, plain unscented bleach (also for water: see the
      safe-water card).
- [ ] A water filter that removes chemicals does **not** always kill germs. For questionable
      water, boil or disinfect first, then filter.

## 🩹 Health & safety
- [ ] First-aid kit + a **real tourniquet** (CAT or SOF-T, not a cheap copy) + gauze.
- [ ] Fire extinguisher (ABC) in the kitchen.
- [ ] Copies of IDs, insurance and prescriptions in a waterproof bag (and on the laptop).
- [ ] Spare glasses/contacts. List each person's medical needs in the comms plan.
- [ ] N95 masks for smoke and dust.

## 🔒 Home security in a long outage
- [ ] Keep doors locked; don't advertise your supplies.
- [ ] Know the neighbours and check on elderly ones. Groups are safer than lone households.
- [ ] A doorstop alarm or door wedge (cheap, no power needed).

## 🔌 Power
- [ ] Power station (e.g. 250–1,000 Wh) + solar panel if you can. Test it now: how long do
      the phones, laptop and a lamp last?
- [ ] Priority: medical devices → phones and radio → lights → laptop → desktop.
- [ ] A desktop PC with a big GPU drains a small station in under an hour. Use the laptop.

## 🚨 Alerts on every phone (free, 5 minutes)
- [ ] Turn on the phone's emergency / public safety alerts:
  - iPhone: Settings → Notifications → bottom.
  - Android: Settings → Safety & emergency → Wireless emergency alerts.
- [ ] Sign up for your county or city's alert texts.
- [ ] Sign up for your power utility's outage texts.
- [ ] Your grid operator's alerts: see [GRID-ALERTS.md](GRID-ALERTS.md).
- [ ] Set the weather radio to your county or area code.

## 📞 Comms (see [COMMS-PLAN.md](COMMS-PLAN.md))
- [ ] Signal + an offline mesh app on every phone, family group made.
- [ ] Paper plan filled in: meeting points, out-of-area contact.
- [ ] Local news radio frequency written on the paper plan.

## 🎒 If you have to leave (go-bag, one per person)
- [ ] Backpack: 3 days of water/food, meds, clothes, power bank, flashlight, cash, document
      copies, first-aid basics, printed cards + comms plan.
- [ ] Know 2 ways out of the area and the meeting points.

---
Buy the 🔴 list first. Food and water can be bought gradually over a week or two.
