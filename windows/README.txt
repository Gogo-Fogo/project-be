EMERGENCY LIBRARY + AI  —  Windows PC
=====================================

Everything here works with NO internet. If the library is on an external drive, it MUST be
plugged in. The shortcuts are on the Desktop.

START
  Double-click "Start Emergency".
    - The "Emergency Help" page opens in the browser. Type a question (or tap an example)
      and press Ask. The AI answers only from the offline library and lists the articles it
      used as [1], [2], [3]; click them to read the full article.
    - "Open library" (top right) opens the whole library (Wikipedia, WikiMed medical
      encyclopedia, survival guides, iFixit repair guides, Wikivoyage).
    - With a graphics card an answer takes about 10 seconds; without one, about a minute.
      "Stop" stops an answer that's taking too long.
    - The menu at the top picks the AI.

QUICK-REFERENCE CARDS (fastest, no AI)
  The "Emergency Cards" shortcut opens short, checked steps for: choking, CPR, severe
  bleeding, burns, heart attack/stroke, food in a power outage, safe water, carbon monoxide.
  PRINT them and keep the paper with the emergency kit ("Print Emergency Cards.html").

FIRST-AID VIDEOS (if they were downloaded)
  "Emergency Videos" on the Desktop. Watch them NOW, not for the first time in an emergency.

SEARCHING THE LIBRARY IN THE BROWSER
  The search box on the library's front page only searches book NAMES. Click a book first
  (e.g. "WikiMed" for medical), then use the search bar at the top.

STOP
  Double-click "Stop Emergency" when finished (closing the browser tab is not enough).

POWER OUT
  A desktop PC uses a lot of power: on a small power station it lasts about an hour.
  Shut it down properly and use the laptop instead.

IMPORTANT
  - The AI can be wrong. For medical questions, read the WikiMed article it cites.
  - Call your emergency number if you can.
  - "Library not found": plug in the drive with the library and start again.
  - Don't switch to the big AI models without the person who set this up: on some PCs
    they can freeze the screen.
