CHOKING
=======
If they can cough hard or talk: let them keep coughing. Stay with them.
If they CANNOT cough, talk or breathe: CALL 911 (or have someone call), then:

ADULT or CHILD over 1 year
  1. Stand behind them. Lean them forward.
  2. Give 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
  3. Give 5 abdominal thrusts: fist just above the belly button, other hand over it,
     pull sharply IN and UP.
  4. Repeat 5 back blows + 5 thrusts until the object comes out or they pass out.
  Pregnant or very large person: do the thrusts on the center of the chest instead.

BABY under 1 year
  1. Lay baby face-DOWN along your forearm, head lower than chest, support the jaw.
  2. Give 5 back blows between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
  3. Turn baby face-UP, head lower than chest.
  4. Give 5 chest pushes: 2 fingers in the center of the chest, just below the nipples,
     about 1.5 inches deep.
  5. Repeat until the object comes out or baby passes out.
  NEVER do belly thrusts on a baby. Don't sweep the mouth blindly.

IF THEY PASS OUT (any age)
  Lay them on their back and start CPR (see the CPR card). Each time you open the
  mouth for breaths, look for the object and take it out only if you can SEE it.
