SEVERE BLEEDING
===============
Spurting, pooling, or soaking through cloth = life-threatening. CALL 911.

  1. PRESS. Put a clean cloth (or your gloved/bagged hand) right on the wound and
     press HARD with both hands. Don't lift it to look.
  2. Soaked through? Put more cloth ON TOP and keep pressing. Don't remove the first layer.
  3. Deep wound in the thigh, armpit, neck or groin: stuff (pack) cloth INTO the wound,
     then press hard on it for at least 3 minutes.
  4. ARM or LEG still bleeding badly with hard pressure? TOURNIQUET:
       - 2-3 inches ABOVE the wound (toward the body), never on a joint.
       - Real tourniquet, or a 2-inch-wide strap/cloth plus a stick: tie, twist the stick
         until the bleeding STOPS, then tie the stick in place.
       - It will hurt. That's expected. Do NOT loosen it.
       - Write the time it went on (on their skin or the tourniquet).
  5. Keep them lying down and warm (blanket). Nothing to eat or drink.

Small cuts: press for 10 minutes, rinse with clean water, cover.
Get help if it won't stop, is gaping, is from an animal or rusty object, or shows
infection later (spreading redness, heat, pus, fever).
