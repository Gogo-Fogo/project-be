FOOD IN A POWER OUTAGE
======================
KEEP THE DOORS SHUT. Every opening lets the cold out.

  Fridge:  keeps food safe about 4 HOURS if unopened.
  Freezer: about 48 HOURS if full, about 24 HOURS if half full (unopened).

After the power comes back
  - Fridge food (meat, fish, eggs, milk, soft cheese, leftovers, cut fruit) that was
    above 40°F (4°C) for more than 2 hours: THROW IT OUT.
  - Freezer food that still has ice crystals or is 40°F (4°C) or colder: safe to refreeze or cook.
  - Never taste food to check it. Smell and looks don't tell you if it's safe.
  - WHEN IN DOUBT, THROW IT OUT.

Usually fine without the fridge for a while: hard cheese, butter, whole fruit and veg,
bread, peanut butter, jam, ketchup, mustard, opened canned fruit juice.

Tips
  - A thermometer in the fridge and freezer tells you for sure.
  - Coolers with ice/frozen packs can save the most important food (meds, baby food).
  - Medicines that need cold (insulin etc.): check the leaflet; most insulin is fine at
    room temperature for weeks.

(Normal storage times like "1-7 days in the fridge" do NOT apply once the fridge is off.)
