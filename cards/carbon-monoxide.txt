CARBON MONOXIDE (CO): the outage killer
========================================
You can't see or smell it. Many outage deaths are from CO, not cold.

NEVER use indoors, in a garage, or near open windows:
  - generators (keep at least 20 feet / 6 m from the house, exhaust pointing away),
  - charcoal or gas grills, camp stoves, propane heaters not made for indoor use,
  - a car running in a garage, even with the door open.
Never heat the home with the oven or stove.

SIGNS: headache, dizziness, weakness, nausea/vomiting, chest pain, confusion,
sleepiness. Several people (or pets) sick at once in the same place = think CO.

IF YOU SUSPECT CO
  1. Get EVERYONE (and pets) outside into fresh air NOW. Don't stop to open windows.
  2. CALL 911 from outside.
  3. Someone passed out and not breathing normally: CPR (see the CPR card), outside.
  4. Don't go back in until the fire department says it's safe.

A battery CO alarm near the bedrooms is the best protection. Test it monthly.
