QUICK-REFERENCE CARDS
=====================
Short, checked instructions for the emergencies where a mistake is dangerous or the
library has no answer. They open instantly, with no AI.

  choking.txt              Choking (adult, child, baby)
  cpr.txt                  CPR (adult, child, baby)
  bleeding.txt             Severe bleeding, tourniquet
  burns.txt                Burns and scalds
  heart-attack-stroke.txt  Heart attack and stroke signs
  power-outage-food.txt    Fridge and freezer food in an outage
  safe-water.txt           Boiling and bleach for drinking water
  carbon-monoxide.txt      Generators, grills and CO poisoning

The question tool shows the matching card first, then the AI answer.
Written Nov 2026 from public guidance (American Red Cross, American Heart Association,
USDA, CDC, EPA). They are not a substitute for a first-aid/CPR class. Take one.

PRINT THESE and keep the paper copy with the emergency kit: paper works with no power.
