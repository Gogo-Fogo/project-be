SAFE DRINKING WATER
===================
Best first: use stored bottled water. Plan 1 gallon (4 liters) per person per day.

BOIL (best method)
  1. Cloudy? Let it settle, then pour it through a clean cloth or coffee filter.
  2. Bring to a ROLLING boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 ft / 2,000 m).
  3. Let it cool. Store covered in clean containers.

BLEACH (if you can't boil)
  Use plain, UNSCENTED household bleach (6% or 8.25%). Not "splash-less", not scented.
                   6% bleach         8.25% bleach
    1 liter        2 drops           2 drops
    1 gallon       8 drops           6 drops
  Double it if the water is cloudy or very cold. Stir, wait 30 MINUTES.
  It should smell slightly of chlorine; if not, add the same dose again, wait 15 min more.

Won't fix: water with fuel, chemicals or flood water from industrial areas.
Boiling and bleach kill germs, they don't remove chemicals. Find another source.

Hidden water at home: water heater tank, ice cubes, canned fruit/veg liquid.
NOT from: toilet bowl, radiators, waterbeds, pools (for drinking).
