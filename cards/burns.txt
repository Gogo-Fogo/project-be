KEYWORDS: burn, scald, scorch, boiling water, hot oil, blister, fire
BURNS
=====
  1. Stop the burning: away from the heat. Clothes on fire: STOP, DROP and ROLL,
     or smother with a heavy blanket.
  2. COOL it under cool (not ice-cold) running water for 20 minutes. Even a while
     after the burn, it still helps. No ice, no butter, no toothpaste, no oil.
  3. Take off rings, watches and clothing near the burn before it swells,
     unless it's stuck to the skin (then leave it).
  4. Cover loosely with plastic wrap (lay it on, don't wrap tight) or a clean,
     non-fluffy cloth.
  5. Don't pop blisters.
  6. Pain: ibuprofen or acetaminophen (paracetamol) as the box says.
  7. Keep the person warm otherwise; big burns make people cold fast.

CALL 911 / GET HELP if:
  - the burn is bigger than their palm, or deep (white, leathery or charred),
  - it's on the face, hands, feet, genitals or a joint, or goes all the way round a limb,
  - it's from electricity or chemicals (chemicals: rinse 20+ minutes first),
  - they breathed in smoke (cough, hoarse voice, soot around nose/mouth),
  - it's a baby, a small child or an elderly person.
