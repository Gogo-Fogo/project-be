CPR (someone collapsed and isn't breathing normally)
====================================================
  1. Tap and shout. No response and not breathing normally (gasping doesn't count)?
  2. CALL 911 (or have someone call). Ask someone to find an AED if there is one.
  3. Lay them on their back on something firm.

ADULT (and teenager)
  - Hands stacked, heel of hand in the CENTER of the chest.
  - Push HARD and FAST: 2 to 2.4 inches deep, 100-120 pushes a minute
    (the beat of "Stayin' Alive"). Let the chest come all the way back up.
  - If you are trained: 30 pushes, then 2 breaths (tilt head back, lift chin,
    pinch nose, 1 second each, watch the chest rise). If not trained: just push, no breaths.

CHILD (1 year to puberty)
  - One or two hands, about 2 inches deep, same speed. 30 pushes, 2 breaths.

BABY (under 1 year)
  - 2 fingers just below the nipple line, about 1.5 inches deep, same speed.
  - 30 pushes, then 2 small puffs covering baby's mouth AND nose.

KEEP GOING until they breathe, help takes over, or an AED is ready.
AED: turn it on and do exactly what it says. Keep pushing while it charges.
Swap with someone every 2 minutes if you can; tired pushes are too shallow.
